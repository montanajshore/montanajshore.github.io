About Me

I am a developmental and cognitive scientist studying the foundations of fairness reasoning and ethical decision-making.

Currently, I am a fourth-year PhD student in the Social Development and Learning Lab, working with Dr. Peter Blake.

My doctoral research is supported by a SSHRC Doctoral Fellowship.

Background

I grew up in Vancouver, British Columbia, before moving to Kingston, Ontario, to pursue my BA in Psychology at Queen’s University. During my undergraduate studies, I completed my honors thesis with Dr. Valerie Kuhlmeier.

After graduating in 2022, I moved to Durham, North Carolina, where I worked as a lab manager for Dr. Tamar Kushnir at Duke University.
